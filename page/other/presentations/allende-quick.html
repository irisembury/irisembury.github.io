# Salvador Allende - only elected with 36 percent of vote in very close election - gave a speech declaring "the people have rejected capitalism" - thought he had a mandate to completely transform the country - immediate started nationalizing many industries - copper mining - other industrial sectors (coal, steel, etc.) - financial system - large private banks - published his agenda for "Chilean socialism" - Chilean economy crashed - wages collapse - shortages of everything - black markets for basic goods - hyper-inflation - the rest of the country united against him - Chilean congress passes laws, constitutional amendments to try and stop him from continuing to nationalize everything - he ignores them, constitutional crisis - majority of the congress supported a motion asking him to be removed from power, calling his government illegal End of page
