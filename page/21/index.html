I've been using it and edits of it across the internet for a long time now, often as a logo or profile picture. I've also come across different versions of it. It's not just that some have been colour-corrected; it looks like different versions were painted. The most obvious difference is the wall behind her, but many small details differ. ||image-span 200 21-6.png Interestingly, if you try and determine its identity, there are two common answers: * **A beauty holding a bird** by Louis Emile Pinel de Grandchamp (1831--1894) * **L'oiseau Chéri** (French for 'lovely bird' or something) by Alexandre Cabanel (1823--1889) Cabanel is the more well-known artist. Even if you don't recognize his name, you'd probably recognize many of his paintings, like [The Birth of Venus](21-7.png)[](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sandro_Botticelli_046.jpg) or [Nymph abducted by a faun](media/21-2.png)[](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nymphe_et_Saty_(Alexandre_Cabanel\)_1860.JPG). Grandchamp doesn't even have a Wikipedia page. What a nobody! But you can find his other works on various art websites, and his name is mentioned on some index pages, such as this [list of Orientialist artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Orientalist_artists) on Wikipedia (his name is included but doesn't link to anything), meaning Western artists who did works with Arabic or Asian influence, as this work does appear to be. If you look at other paintings by Cabanel, they don't resemble this one in theme or style. Cabanel was more interested in classical European style and specialized in portraiture and European myth. Whereas Pinel de Grandchamp's other work was more focused on slice of life and more often featured foreign cultures. I also feel Cabanel being a more noteworthy painter means if it were his, it would be easier for me to verify that with a higher degree of certainty. The fact there's a dispute about its origin adds plausibility to it being a painting by somebody lesser known, occasionally mistakenly attributed to somebody else. So I list this file as *A beauty holding a bird* by Louis Emile Pinel de Grandchamp, not by Cabanel. # In a vintage tavern ||image-float 21-2.jpg I randomly this picture one day while searching through Wikimedia commons.
