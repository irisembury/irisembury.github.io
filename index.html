I'm a small writer and YouTuber from Ontario, Canada. I set up this website to be a convergence point for everything I do, to host content in a way I have full control over, and for some unique pages that didn't fit well anywhere else.

I'm trying to help push back against an online ecosystem increasingly dominated by extremism, conspiracy theorism, racism, and misinformation. To that end, I oppose both right-populism (Trump, Farage, Orbán, etc.) and the far-left. I stand for liberal society (liberty, democracy, and rule of law), which totalitarian movements like Nazism and Communism both seek to subvert and destroy. I know my enemies.

I'm an Atlanticist and pro-NATO. I believe the Ukraine war is the most important thing happening in the world. In Canada, I favour raising military spending, expanding our oil and gas infrastructure, lowering immigration, and pushing for parliamentary and electoral reform. I feel optimistic about Canada's future.

I write about politics from a traditional liberal-conservative perspective. I like Christian democrats. I like strong centrists like Macron and Carney. I'm politically flexible as I believe character is often more important than policy. I could be called a moderate, a Red Tory, or social democrat depending on the circumstances.

To understand Western politics, I recommend reading about the ideas of Hobbes, Smith, Burke, and Rawls. I don't like Locke or Kant.

I'm a trans woman, so if you're writing about me in a formal context then you're supposed to use feminine pronouns, otherwise the intergalactic police will come to your house and cancel you (wee woo, wee woo).
